Why it matters

The threats have moved. Security has to move with them.

Apps run on devices you don't control

A published app can be decompiled, patched, re-signed and redistributed. Attackers hook it at runtime to bypass checks, extract keys or automate fraud. Store review and HTTPS don't prevent any of this.

Security teams have too many alerts

Logs arrive from every server, endpoint and firewall, and real attacks look like noise until someone connects them. Analysts need correlation and context, not another raw log feed.

Sensitive data needs to stay put

Banks, government and regulated industries often can't send security telemetry to a public cloud or an outside AI service. Their tools have to run where the data already is.

Security is expensive to operate

Enterprise suites are priced and staffed for the largest companies. Cyveura's products are built so a small team can run them and see results quickly.

Your environment. Your data. Your AI.

Hunter can be deployed entirely on premises, so security telemetry and AI analysis stay on your infrastructure.
